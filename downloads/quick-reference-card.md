# Quick Reference Card — Resume Credibility

One-page summary of the whole open-source ecosystem. Print it, keep it next to your screen.

---

## Fix order (do these in sequence)

1. **Summary** — name role, years, stack/domain, one recent scope
2. **Top bullet of recent role** — artifact + collaborator (recruiter-askable noun)
3. **Per-bullet pass** — remove banned verbs (leveraged, spearheaded, drove)
4. **Anti-buzzword pass** — delete Tier 1 phrases
5. **Tone pass** — mix lengths, vary lead words
6. **Credibility pass** — flag invented metrics + scope inflation

---

## Verb hierarchy (use these freely)

Built, Wrote, Designed, Migrated, Refactored, Rewrote, Split, Merged, Added, Removed, Replaced, Shipped, Owned, Carried, Ran, Documented, Reviewed, Tested, Deployed, Killed, Deprecated, Mentored, Hired, Onboarded.

---

## Banned (any use = AI tell)

passionate · results-oriented · proven track record · dynamic · team player · go-getter · leverage · spearheaded · drove · cross-functional · cutting-edge · robust · scalable · innovative · world-class · mission-critical.

---

## Six-second recruiter scan

Title → top bullet → previous title → first two words of every bullet. Optimize that path.

---

## ATS in one line

Text-selectable PDF, one column, 8–12 role keywords inside real bullets, exact tool names.
